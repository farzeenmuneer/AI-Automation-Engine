1. OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
The purpose of this User Acceptance Testing (UAT) guide is to validate that 
the e-commerce checkout flow is functionally stable, provides a seamless 
user experience, and meets business requirements.

This guide is designed for Business Stakeholders and Product Managers to 
independently verify the application before production deployment.
